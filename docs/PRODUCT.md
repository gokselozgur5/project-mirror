# Product definition

## Promise

Project Mirror makes a character feel present across a screen and a small
physical form without hiding how it works or surrendering private sensor data.

## First experience

A participant approaches the desk. The camera estimates their direction. The
screen avatar and pan-tilt head turn together. The character greets them, can be
interrupted naturally, and later recalls only information the participant chose
to preserve.

## Initial users

- Builders learning edge AI, robotics, and real-time applications.
- Streamers who want a reactive desk character.
- Exhibitions and reception desks with explicit visitor notice.
- Researchers prototyping embodied interaction without a full humanoid robot.

## Non-goals

- Hidden recording or biometric identification.
- Medical, therapeutic, emergency, or safety-critical advice.
- Pretending the character is a human being.
- General-purpose autonomous navigation or manipulation.
- Cloud dependency for basic perception and motion.

## Success measures

- A new developer runs the simulator in under ten minutes.
- Physical motion stops within the configured heartbeat timeout.
- Pose commands remain inside mechanical limits under malformed input.
- Five-minute sessions do not accumulate latency or memory.
- Users can inspect and delete stored memories.
